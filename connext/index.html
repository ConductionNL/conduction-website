External tools we integrate

Open source we use, not own.

Most Conduction integrations are with the standard Nextcloud apps already on the home screen, Mail, Files, Calendar, Contacts, Notes, Deck. The set below is different. These are external open-source projects we did not build. We host them next to Nextcloud as ExApps (separate processes the workspace runs and exposes through its UI), or call them through OpenConnector adapters. Reusing them is cheaper than rewriting them, and it keeps the credit where it belongs.

Why through the app store?

Each ExApp is packaged for the Nextcloud app store, so installing one feels the same as installing any other Nextcloud app. We ship the option because sovereignty without choice is just a slogan. Like Nextcloud Talk with its tight workspace fit? Use it. Prefer a federated chat like Matrix? Use that instead. Same login, same workspace, your call on the engine.
