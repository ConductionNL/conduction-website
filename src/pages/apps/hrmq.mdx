      title="Leave and verzuim, with Poortwachter milestones."
    >
      Leave requests carry a statutory-minimum leave balance (4× contractual weekly hours per BW 7:634, lapsing 1 July the following year) checked by three machine-checkable rules. Sickness cases track the Wet-verbetering-poortwachter milestones — probleemanalyse in week 6, plan van aanpak in week 8, the 42-weken ziekmelding at UWV, the eerstejaarsevaluatie in week 52 — as derived, rule-audited dates, never medical data. Approved absence syncs to a plain Nextcloud calendar showing only "Afwezig" or "Verlof", never a reason or diagnosis.
    </FeatureItem>
    <FeatureItem
      icon={<svg viewBox="0 0 24 24"><circle cx="12" cy="8" r="4"/><path d="M5 21v-2a4 4 0 0 1 4-4h6a4 4 0 0 1 4 4v2"/></svg>}
      title="Employee and manager self-service."
    >
      A Mijn HR surface shows each employee only their own records — leave, timesheets, expenses, payslips. Managers get team-scoped approval queues and dashboard KPIs filtered to their own team, kept honest by a rule that checks the team stamp against the org chart.
    </FeatureItem>
    <FeatureItem
      icon={<svg viewBox="0 0 24 24"><circle cx="12" cy="12" r="9"/><path d="M12 6v6l4 2"/></svg>}
      title="Time & attendance under the Arbeidstijdenwet."

      Per-employee clock records with stored worked hours, checked against three ATW rules: at least 11 hours of daily rest between working days (art. 5:3), at most 12 hours per shift (art. 5:7), and the statutory break tiers (art. 5:4). A MijnAanwezigheid self-service page shows the employee's own clock history.
    </FeatureItem>
  </FeatureList>

Candidate PII lives in one object with one retention clock — the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens sollicitatie-bewaartermijn (delete within 4 weeks of rejection, up to 1 year with explicit opt-in) is a stored, rule-checked fact.
    </FeatureItem>

      A review per employee per cycle carries rating, sterktes, ontwikkelpunten, and agreements inside one dossier — no separate goal entity to keep in sync. Separation of duties is enforced on vaststellen, and a rule checks that a finalised review with a rating and agreements actually forms an ontslagdossier under BW 7:669.
    </FeatureItem>
